Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Professional Headshot – AI Photo (“Headshot App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This document explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the Professional Headshot – AI Photo mobile application and related services (the “App” or “Service”).

⸻

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information  
The App does not require account registration and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other direct identifiers to create a user account.

1.2 User Content (Photos)  
When you use the App, you may upload photos to generate professional headshots and document-style photos (e.g., passport/ID/DV Lottery formats).

Your uploaded photos are processed solely to provide the output you requested.

Uploaded photos may be temporarily stored and transmitted to secure servers to process your request and deliver results.

1.3 Generated Outputs  
The App produces AI-processed images (generated outputs). Generated images may be stored:
• Locally on your device if you choose to save them, and/or  
• Temporarily on our servers to deliver the result to you.

1.4 Usage Data (Analytics & Diagnostics)  
We collect limited, anonymized usage and diagnostics data to improve stability and performance, such as:
• App interactions (feature usage)  
• Crash reports  
• Device type and operating system version  

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for these purposes.

⸻

2. How We Use Information

We use information solely to:
• Process your photo and deliver the requested generated result  
• Operate and maintain the Service  
• Improve app reliability and performance (analytics/diagnostics)  
• Prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse  

We do not sell user photos or personal data, and we do not use your photos for advertising.

⸻

3. Third-Party Processing & Infrastructure

To provide AI-powered image processing, the App uses third-party infrastructure and AI services.

3.1 AI Processing Provider (Google Gemini / Nano Banana)  
The App uses Google Gemini (Nano Banana / Nano Banana PRO workflow) for image processing.

When you initiate a generation request, we may transmit:
• The photo you uploaded  
• Your selected style/format parameters (e.g., aspect ratio, preset selection)  
• Technical metadata required to process the request (e.g., request ID, webhook ID)

We do not transmit your contacts, messages, or other unrelated device data.

3.2 Cloud Infrastructure (Firebase / Google Cloud)  
We use Firebase services (including Firebase Cloud Functions, Firebase Storage, Firebase Firestore, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics) to:
• Receive upload requests and securely relay them to the AI processing provider  
• Temporarily store photos and generated outputs needed to complete delivery  
• Send notifications about completion (where enabled)  
• Collect anonymized analytics and crash diagnostics

3.3 Purpose Limitation  
Your photos are transmitted only when you actively request generation. Data is used exclusively to:
• Process the requested headshot/document photo  
• Return the generated result to you

We do not use user photos for profiling, targeted advertising, or marketing.

3.4 Retention and Deletion  
We aim to minimize retention:
• Uploaded photos are kept only as long as necessary to process your request and deliver the output.  
• After processing is completed and the result is delivered, uploaded photos are deleted from our storage.  
• Generated outputs may be temporarily stored to allow you to download/save them, and may be deleted after delivery/expiration of temporary links.

Important: Third-party providers may process data according to their own terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of Google and Firebase for additional details.

3.5 Model Training  
We do not use your uploaded photos to train our own models. We do not build biometric profiles or facial recognition templates.

⸻

4. Face Data

Your uploaded photos may contain facial imagery. We do NOT:
• Extract or store facial geometry templates  
• Perform facial recognition or identify you  
• Sell or share facial imagery for advertising or tracking  

Facial imagery is used only to generate the result you requested and is deleted from our servers after processing/delivery as described above.

⸻

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

⸻

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
